The 6-Step End-to-End Product Launch Framework

Launching a consumer product in India requires a disciplined operational methodology that integrates market validation, chemical formulation, factory auditing, luxury packaging design, regulatory filings, and aggressive marketplace listing execution.

STEP 01

Discovery & Market Validation

We perform comprehensive category benchmarking, competitor mapping, margin architecture analysis, and consumer pain point identification to validate high-margin product opportunities before upfront capital investment.

STEP 02

Formulation & Factory Sourcing

Access pre-audited OEM and ODM manufacturing laboratories with certified WHO-GMP, ISO 22716, and AYUSH accreditations for custom cosmetic, perfume, herbal, and supplement formulations.

STEP 03

Luxury Brand Identity & Packaging

We create iconic naming, luxury structural packaging dielines, European glass component sourcing, label typography, and unboxing aesthetics that command premium retail price points.

STEP 04

Regulatory Compliance & Trademarking

Comprehensive legal protection: Trademark class searches & filings, FDA cosmetic licenses, AYUSH certification, FSSAI supplement registrations, GST documentation, and barcode issuance.

STEP 05

E-Commerce & Marketplace Launch

High-converting digital execution: Complete onboarding on Amazon India (FBA & Brand Store A+ content), Flipkart Smart Fulfillment, Nykaa beauty curation, and custom headless Shopify storefronts.

STEP 06

Growth & Commercial Scaling

Post-launch scaling: Performance marketing funnels, influencer seeding activations, unit economics optimization, retail channel distribution, and product line extensions for sustainable cash flow.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What does a Product Launch Company in India do?

A Product Launch Company manages the complete, turnkey lifecycle of introducing a new product into the market. This includes market validation, chemical formulation, factory sourcing, luxury packaging design, legal compliance (trademarks, FDA, AYUSH, FSSAI), and multi-channel launch across Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, and D2C Shopify websites.

How long does it take to launch a product in India?

With Banega Brand's structured 6-step framework, product launches typically take between 45 to 90 days from initial discovery to live marketplace sales, depending on formulation complexity, stability testing, and packaging customization.

How much capital is required to start a D2C brand in India?

Initial investment generally ranges from ₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs depending on the chosen category, Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), primary bottle tooling, and marketing launch budget. Our Investment Guide helps founders optimize capital allocation.

Do I need an existing formula or factory to work with Banega Brand?

No. Over 70% of founders begin with only an idea or category preference. We handle custom formulation, lab sampling, factory audits, and production oversight through our verified manufacturer network.
